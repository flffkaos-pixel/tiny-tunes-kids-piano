Privacy Policy for Tiny Tunes: Kids Piano

Last updated: September 15, 2026

Tiny Tunes: Kids Piano is a children's piano music game. We take children's privacy seriously.

Data collection

The app itself does not collect personal data, has no user accounts, no analytics, and no in-app purchases. The musical game works offline.

Advertising (AdMob)

This app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. Because the app is directed at children, ads are configured as child-directed, G-rated and non-personalized in line with the Google Play Families Policy. Google may process device identifiers (such as the advertising ID) solely to serve these safe, non-personalized ads and prevent invalid activity. See Google's Privacy Policy and AdMob & children's privacy. Parents can reset the device advertising ID in Android system settings.

Permissions

The app requests the Vibrate permission, used only to give gentle haptic feedback when keys are tapped. No data leaves the device.

Children's privacy

The app is directed at children under 6 with parental guidance and complies with the Google Play Families Policy. Only Google-certified, child-safe advertising is used; no personalized ads or interest-based tracking.

Contact

Questions: please contact us via the Google Play store listing email.

All song melodies in this app are traditional public-domain tunes, synthesized on-device. No lyrics are displayed.
